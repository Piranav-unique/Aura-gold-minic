## 🎁 Refer & Earn

Each savings scheme has its own referral code. When a friend joins with your code, their scheme is set automatically. You earn a cash reward in your wallet once they make their first qualifying purchase.

<div align="center">

| Scheme | Your Reward | Friend's First Purchase |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **1 g** | **₹150** | ₹100 or more |
| **5 g** | **₹350** | ₹1,000 or more |
| **10 g** | **₹550** | ₹2,000 or more |

</div>

Copy your code or share a link in one tap. Your referrals, earnings and wallet balance are all tracked in one place.

## 🧭 Admin Console

The same app includes a back-office console for the Aurum team. What each person sees depends on their role and permissions.

| Module | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| 📊 **Executive Dashboard** | Role-based KPIs and business overview for admins, managers and employees |
| 💸 **Sell Requests** | Review customer sell requests, then approve them with an automatic bank payout or reject them |
| 👛 **User Wallets** | Search customers, view their balances, and drill into complete transaction ledgers |
| 🧮 **Payment Settlements** | Reconcile online payments against Razorpay records |
| 🏛️ **Metal Inventory** | Manage gold and silver stock pools, restocks and reservations, with low-stock alerts |
| 📦 **Inventory & Suppliers** | Physical stock items, stock movements and supplier records |
| 👥 **Customers** | A complete customer directory with management tools |
| 🔁 **Workflows** | Approval workflows with escalation rules |
| 📑 **Reports** | Business analytics with export to CSV, Excel and PDF |
| 🕵️ **Audit Logs** | A searchable activity history with export |
| 🔐 **Users, Roles & Permissions** | Fine-grained access control for every team member |
| 🗂️ **Account Deletions** | Process customer data-deletion requests |
| 🏢 **Organization Profile** | Manage company details |
